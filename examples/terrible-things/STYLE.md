# House style: All the Terrible Things We Didn't Think

One game, one hand. Every sprite and CG follows this sheet. `ASSETS.md` says *what*
to draw; this says *how*. The reference implementation is `chars/suzu/*.svg`
(generated by one Python script so shared parts stay byte-identical). Open it next
to your work and match its finish.

**The look:** bright, clean anime / visual-novel cel art. Flat colour plus one
shadow tone, crisp *coloured* line art, big glossy eyes, saturated light, lively
faces. Glass is the motif: clear (never grey), with threads of coloured light
inside and a rainbow rim. Light comes from the **upper left** (shadows fall to
screen-right and down) unless a scene has its own source (kiln, lanterns, the
Heart).

---

## 1. Canvas and scale (sprites)

- Every file of a character: `width="760" height="1040" viewBox="0 0 760 1040"`,
  transparent background (no backdrop rect).
- The engine draws sprites at natural size on the 1920×1080 stage, bottom edge on
  the bottom of the screen: **stage y = canvas y + 40**. The figure is cut by the
  bottom edge at mid-thigh.
- Centre the body on **x = 380**. The head may lean a few degrees, but the
  figure's axis stays on 380.
- The textbox covers stage y ≥ 810, i.e. **canvas y ≥ 770**. Hands, props and
  anything the story mentions must sit above canvas y ≈ 745.
- **All heads are the same size** (the cast are 17–18): skull top to chin =
  **200 px**, face width at the eye line = **184 px** (x 288–472 when centred).
  Inside the head: eye line **E = skull top + 120**, chin = skull top + 200.
  Height differences come only from where the head sits (≈5.5 px per cm):

| character | height | skull top | eye line E | chin | silhouette extras | shoulders |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hinata | 172 cm | **62** | 182 | 262 | bun + bandana up to y ≈ 8 | ~310 px, broad |
| Nemu | 162 cm, slouched | **112** (head tilted ~6°) | 232 | 312 | cowlick up to y ≈ 70 | ~270 px, coat makes it boxy |
| Nagi | 155 cm | **148** | 268 | 348 | flat bob, top ≈ 134 | ~250 px, neat |
| Suzu | 145 cm | **210** | 330 | 410 | updo + pins up to y ≈ 30 | ~220 px, tiny |

  "Skull top" is the top of the head under the hair; hair adds 12–16 px. Line
  them up bottom-aligned and Hinata's face is a head higher than Suzu's, while
  Suzu's crown of pins still reaches almost as high as Hinata's bun.
- **Body proportions** (≈6 heads tall, anime-slender): shoulders at chin + 50,
  bust at chin + 150, waist/obi at chin + 190…290, canvas bottom = mid-thigh.
  Hands are about 0.5 of a head long (≈100 px) and drawn with care: they are
  half of each pose.

## 2. Line work

- **Coloured lines, never black.** Each material is outlined in a dark,
  saturated shade of its own colour: hair in its darkest tone; skin `#b9705f`;
  white cloth `#6d6a9e`; glass `#7f8fd6`.
- **Weights:** outer silhouette of every part **2.6 px**; face outline 2.2 px;
  inner folds, hair strands, seams **1.4–2 px at 0.5–0.8 opacity**; pattern
  details 1.2 px. `stroke-linejoin="round"` and `stroke-linecap="round"`
  everywhere.
- Lashes and brows are **filled tapered shapes**, never plain strokes.
- No outline on blush, highlights, glows, sparkles or light threads.

## 3. Colour and shading

- **Cel shading:** base + **one shadow tone** as hard-edged shapes placed for
  upper-left light, plus at most one small deep accent in the darkest folds.
  Base fills may carry a gentle 2–3-stop vertical gradient (lighter at top);
  shadows are flat (or flat with opacity). No airbrushed shading on skin or
  cloth.
- **Highlights are hard-edged:** hair gets one jagged "angel ring" sheen band of a
  lighter tone plus 1–2 thin white strokes; satin (obi) gets one long soft band.
  Only light effects (glass, glow, blush, tears, bead light) use blur filters.
- **Skin (everyone):** base `#fde8dc`, shadow `#f6cfc2` (bang shadow across the
  forehead, jaw shadow onto the neck), deep `#eab3a6` under the chin, line
  `#b9705f`, nose `#d3907f` (one short stroke) with a tiny white highlight.
- **Blush:** radial-gradient ellipse `#ff8fa3` (centre 0.55 → edge 0) about
  56×20 px, centred under each eye at **y = E + 40** (keep it clear of the blink
  patch, §7). Strong blush (proud, angry, tears, flustered): the `#ff6f8c`
  gradient (0.8 → 0) plus 3 short diagonal hatch strokes per cheek (1.6 px
  `#ff5a7a` at 0.55, each with a 0.9 px white twin).
- Saturated, not muddy: shadows lean cool (violet/blue), highlights warm.

## 4. Faces

Template for a head centred on x = 380 with eye line E (table above). Left eye
centre (screen left) **x = 334**, right eye **x = 426**. Build one eye and mirror
it about x = 380.

- **Eye opening** (white of the eye): ~60 × 52 px, top lid peaking near E − 30,
  bottom at E + 26; the outer corner sits a little higher than the inner one.
  Sclera gradient `#d9d6ee` (top) → white at 45 %.
- **Iris:** ellipse rx 21, ry 25 clipped to the opening; it fills most of it.
  Vertical gradient: darkest at the top (under the lid), saturated middle, a
  light glowing bottom. Pupil ellipse rx 8 ry 11 (Suzu has none). A darker
  1.6 px iris rim, a 0.3-opacity lid-shadow band across the top third, and a
  soft light crescent at the bottom.
- **Suzu's glass eyes** replace iris + pupil with a clear marble: radial
  gradient white (upper left) → `#d4c4f7` → `#9a80dc` rim, a soft blurred violet
  plume, one thin curling filament of smoke (blurred `#7d3fd1` core under a
  1 px `#ecdcff` line), a pink-white caustic glow at the bottom, a rainbow inner
  ring and the usual highlights. The marbles sit 4 px toward screen-left of the
  eye centres, so her gaze lands just past the viewer.
- **Highlights**, on top, in this order: a big white ellipse rx 7 ry 8.5 rotated
  −18° at the iris's upper left; a small white circle r 3.2 lower right; a dot
  r 1.6 lower left. Same positions in every open-eyed expression.
- **Upper lash line:** a filled shape 5–7 px thick in the middle, tapering to the
  inner corner, with an outer-corner flick and 1–2 short lash spikes; colour =
  darkest hair tone. **Lower lash:** a 1.7 px stroke on the outer 60 %, lighter.
  **Crease:** a 1.5 px skin-line stroke ~10 px above the lash line at 0.75.
- **Brows:** thin tapered fills (2–4 px), hair-line colour, drawn **after the
  bangs** so they read over the hair, each with a soft pale halo underneath
  (same path, `stroke="#f6d3dc"` 3.2 px at 0.55, no fill) so a dark brow stays
  readable on dark bangs. Neutral brows sit ~24 px above the lid peak. Every
  brow in every expression stays **outside the blink patch** (§7).
- **Nose:** one short 1.7 px stroke from E + 30 to E + 39. **Mouth** at E + 52…62. Closed
  mouths: 2 px strokes in `#9a4842` with a tiny darker corner tick. Open mouths:
  filled `#8a2f3a`, `#f08a96` tongue, 1.8 px `#6b2430` outline, upper teeth as a
  white band when they show.
- **Expressions should be big and readable at 50 % size.** Use the extras:
  four-point sparkles for smugness, a sweat drop (pale blue, white highlight) for
  panic, short tension lines for surprise, a pink "anger" tick for comic
  indignation, a soft shadow under the eyes for tired. Comic marks (anger
  veins, sweat drops) only on expressions that are never used seriously.
- **Tears** are clear water that catches the light like the glass: inside the
  eye (clipped to the eye opening) a pale-cyan pool along the lower lid at 0.55
  with a crisp white meniscus line, plus 1–2 extra iris sparkles; on the cheek
  a stream that starts thin at the outer third of the lower lid and widens as
  it runs down, filled with a white → `#bfe9ff` gradient (0.8–0.95 opacity),
  1.1 px `#7fcff6` outline, a white highlight down one side and a short
  rainbow fleck, ending in a drop; one or two loose droplets below. Keep
  streams on the cheek, not over the hair.
- **Hair across the face:** draw the crown and the bangs as **one path**, so no
  line crosses the forehead (no "helmet" line). Bangs are 5–7 **clumps with
  curved sides and soft points**, of varied length, notches only 20–35 px deep,
  each clump with one lighter strand line; never a regular sawtooth. The bang
  shadow on the forehead follows the bangs' edge, offset ~3 px right and
  10 px down, with the tips rounded off.

## 5. Glass (the motif)

Glass on bodies, statues, beads and pins is drawn the same way everywhere:

1. **Body of glass:** almost clear. Fill white → pale lavender at **0.10–0.25
   opacity** so whatever is behind shows through. Where glass meets skin it turns
   frosty (white ~0.6) over ~25 px, then skin fades in (gradient masks, no hard
   seam).
2. **Contour:** 2 px `#7f8fd6`, plus a **2 px white inner highlight** along the
   lit (upper-left) edges and a **soft dark-blue inner edge** (`#4a5aa8`, ~6 px,
   0.2 opacity, blurred) so the edges read thick.
3. **Rainbow rim:** a 1.6–2 px stroke with the spectral gradient
   (`#ff7aa8 → #ffd36e → #7ff0c0 → #7ab8ff → #c69bff`) along the shadow-side
   edge, 0.8 opacity.
4. **Threads of light:** 2–4 wavy strokes inside, like ink in water: a blurred
   (σ 2) 4–6 px glow stroke in a bead colour at 0.5–0.7 opacity with a 1–1.4 px
   bright core on top. Pinks, cyans, golds, violets.
5. **Glints:** 1–3 small white four-point stars or dots, and one sharp white
   reflection streak.
6. Never cracked, never grey, never gory. Glass is beautiful.

**Glass hands** (Suzu; reuse the construction in CGs): build each hand in its
own local frame (wrist at the origin, fingers along +y), then place it with one
`translate rotate` and mirror it for the other side. The wrist comes out of the
sleeve opening (an ellipse with the violet lining inside, perpendicular to the
forearm); clip the hand to *that ellipse ∪ everything beyond it*, so the wrist
only shows inside the opening. Skin → glass is a gradient **fill** on the hand
shape (skin opaque at the wrist, transparent ~24 px later), then a frost
gradient, then the clear glass fill; don't use masks for it (§8). Palms up:
we see the palm, the thumb on the outer side, the four fingers together and a
little curled, with finger separations, two palm creases and small white pads
at the fingertips. A soft lavender glow behind both hands.

**Beads** (fingernail size): circle with a radial gradient (white at upper left →
saturated rim), rim stroke in a darker shade of the same hue, a white highlight
ellipse and dot, and a soft blurred glow of the same colour behind (they glow
from inside). Bead palette: ruby `#ff4d6d`, cobalt `#3f6dff`, sea green
`#2fc59a`, honey `#ffc23e`, violet `#a66bff`, rose `#ff7fc4`, aqua `#46d9ff`,
smoke `#b8b0c8`. Hinata's beads are all amber `#ffb02e` (rim `#c86a10`).

## 7. Expressions and the blink overlay

- Body, hair and every non-face element are **byte-identical** across a
  character's expression files. Generate them from one script.
- **Eyes stay in exactly the same place** in every expression. Change only lid
  shape, iris size/visibility, brows, mouth, blush and extras. Never move or
  tilt the head per expression; suggest a bowed head with lowered lids and brows.
- Put the head in one `<g transform="…">` (a small tilt like
  `rotate(-2 380 420)` is fine) and reuse the identical transform in
  `blink.svg`.

**blink.svg** (same canvas, transparent everywhere except the eyes). Use
**clipPaths, not masks**: WebKit (Safari, and the macOS previewer) draws a light
fringe along the edge of a masked group, feathered or not, which shows as a
glowing outline over the hair on every blink.

1. **Blink patch** (one per eye, mirrored) = every expression's eye shapes
   (sclera, upper lash, flick, lower lash, closed-eye arcs, tear pools) sampled
   to points → convex hull → offset outward by **5 px** with round joins.
   `gen_suzu.py` has the helpers (`sample_path`, `hull`, `offset_hull`); copy
   them. It must not reach any brow, the nose, the blush centre or the mouth.
2. Group A, clipped to the patch: redraw **exactly** what lies under the eyes
   in the base art (face skin, bang shadow, jaw shade, base blush, nose), then
   the **closed eyes**: a 5–6 px filled lash curve that sags gently (◡) from
   inner to outer corner, a small flick and 1–2 lashes at the outer corner, and
   a short 1.3 px lower-lid line, same colours as the open lash.
3. Group B, clipped to the patch grown by **7 px** (2 px more): redraw the hair
   that crosses the eyes (crown + bangs, side locks) exactly as in the base
   art. Because the hair clip is larger, the hair is fully opaque where the
   skin clip's anti-aliased edge is, so no seam appears (two layers clipped by
   the same edge leave a light hairline).
4. Keep every brow (including its halo) ≥ 2 px outside the 7 px patch; with
   brows that low, move them up rather than shrinking the patch.
5. Check by compositing blink over every expression and diffing against the
   expression alone: the only differences should be the eyes themselves; no
   white, iris or lash may peek out and no brow may be cut. (On expressions
   with a strong blush, the top rim of the blush lightening for the 0.13 s of
   a blink is acceptable.)

## 8. Hygiene

- Pure SVG: gradients, paths, filters, clipPaths, masks. No `<image>`, no
  external refs, no `<text>`, no scripts. Under ~200 KB per file.
- Prefix every id with a character tag (`su-`, `na-`, `hi-`, `ne-`, `cg-`) so
  nothing collides when files are inlined together.
- Masks only for soft decorative fades that don't have to line up with
  anything (and never with a blur filter inside them); for skin → glass and
  similar fades use gradient fills with `stop-opacity`.
- Preview: `qlmanage -t -s 1600 -o <dir> file.svg` writes `file.svg.png`, but it
  scales the **width** to the size and crops to a square (a 760×1040 sprite
  loses everything below canvas y ≈ 760), and it renders small canvases at only
  ~2.7× their size. To see the whole figure, zoom on the face, or stack
  blink.svg over an expression, wrap the sprite markup in a large square SVG
  with nested `<svg viewBox="…">` panels and rename ids per panel. Ready-made
  helpers live in `/private/tmp/claude-501/showcase-assets/suzu/`:
  `preview.py OUT.png [--bg C] [--crop x,y,w,h] [--cols N] a.svg b.svg+blink.svg`,
  `stage.py OUT.png --bg bg/x.svg sprite.svg@0.5 …` (1920×1080 mock with the
  textbox) and `pngdiff.py a.png b.png diff.png` (pixel diff, no PIL needed).
- Check every sprite on the stage mock over a real background, over a dark
  and a sky-blue fill, and at 50 % size: the silhouettes must read at a glance
  (neat vertical Nagi, tiny bell-shaped Suzu under a crown of pins, tall broad
  Hinata with a bun, long slouched Nemu in a big coat).

## 9. CGs

- 1920×1080, same face construction as the sprites (scale the face template, keep
  its proportions, lash and highlight recipe and line colours; line weights scale
  with the face, ~3–4 px outer lines for a face twice sprite size).
- Suzu in CGs: plum updo (two side loops and a top knot tied with a blue
  dotted cloth, side wings, two long front locks ending in soft curls) with the
  twelve coloured glass pins; glass-marble eyes with a curl of violet smoke and no
  pupils (lit pink in `hands.svg`); clear glass hands with light threads; white
  kimono with blue bellflowers and very long sleeves; pale violet obi, pink
  obiage, plum cord with a glass bead, and a big violet bow at the back.
- Night scenes: keep the line colours but darken the fills with a blue-violet
  multiply layer; light sources (lanterns, beads, the Heart) add soft blurred
  glows and rim-light the nearest edges in the light's colour.
